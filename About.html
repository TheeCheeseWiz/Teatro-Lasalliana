TEATRO Lasalliana

TEATRO Lasalliana (TLS) is one of the seven Performing Arts Groups (PAGs) under the supervision of the Cultural Arts Office and serves as the institutionally recognized premier theatre organization on campus.

TLS champions theatre as a collaborative and transformative art form, consistently producing individuals shaped by brilliance, discipline, and passion—not only for the stage but also for their chosen vocations.

For over three decades, TLS has continued to grow and inspire, spreading awareness of life and society under its enduring motto: “Go for Truth.”
